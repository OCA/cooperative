Usage
=====

On the company, two new toggles 'Display National Number' and 'Require
National Number' are added. In order to expose the functionality of this
module, you must enable these toggles for every company that wishes to
use it. 'Display' shows the field and allows it to be filled in.
'Require' makes the field mandatory.
